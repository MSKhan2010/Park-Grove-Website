The Park Grove story

Our Story

Providing quality care with dignity and respect since 1985.

A place to feel known

Care rooted in comfort, character and community.

Park Grove combines experienced care with the warmth and familiarity of home, supporting every resident as an individual.

01

A Legacy of Care

Park Grove was established in July 1985 and has been under the ownership of Kevin and Una Banks since March 1997, bringing decades of experience in the care sector.

We remain committed to maintaining and improving the quality of our service. Policies, procedures and working practices are kept under regular review.

02

Comfort & Facilities

Registered for 32 residents, our rooms span the ground and first floors, supported by lift access. Bathrooms include a wet room and an easy walk-in modern bath.

A nurse-call facility is available in every room. Residents can enjoy two dining rooms, four lounges, free Wi-Fi and accessible technology for keeping in touch.

03

Community & Lifestyle

Located on the main A59, Park Grove is close to village shops, the post office, bank and library. Bus and rail connections make visits easier for family and friends.

Residents enjoy a varied programme of events within the home, together with links to local clubs, churches, parks and community facilities.

04

Make It Your Own

Residents are welcome to bring personal belongings such as a favourite chair, television, ornaments, shelving and pictures. Room decoration can also be changed.

Where possible, we will accommodate larger items so that each room feels individual, comforting and genuinely like home.
